# Sortify

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
android

## Stack
Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, Android Media3.

## Product Purpose
Convert the supplied React Native music player into a native Kotlin Android app, retaining music discovery, search, playback, personal playlists, favourites, downloads, and language preferences.

## Operating Context
No account is required (confirmed by the user). Personal library and preferences live on the device. Streaming and discovery use a configurable compatible Saavn API; availability depends on that external service.

## Brand Commitments
Sortify name and supplied green S mark. The supplied Spotify showcase establishes dark forest-green surfaces, bright green playback actions, prominent artwork, rounded controls, and persistent mini-player navigation. Extend this design to all remaining app screens.

## Evidence on Hand
Original source: Sortify-Music-App-main/. Original assets and reference images: docs/references/.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve quality selection, playback outside the activity with system media controls, last session history, offline downloads, and editable language preferences. Do not imply that Google or Apple sign-in is connected. No account service was supplied or requested.
